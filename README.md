Centralized Grid-Connected Solar PV
Project Overview

Complete thermal, thermodynamic, and economic analysis of a centralized grid-connected rooftop solar PV system using ANSYS Workbench, linking thermal simulation results with energy balance calculations and a realistic ROI assessment under Egyptian conditions.

Project Contents

ANSYS steady-state thermal simulation of a solar PV module

Temperature distribution and heat loss analysis

Thermodynamic energy balance and efficiency evaluation

Annual energy yield estimation

Techno-economic analysis and ROI calculation for residential rooftop PV

Tools Used

ANSYS Workbench (Steady-State Thermal)

Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer principles

Excel-based engineering calculations

Engineering material property data
